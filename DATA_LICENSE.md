# Terms for generated atmosphere grids and payloads

The atmosphere grids generated by the Space-FALCON Lab and distributed in this
repository or its releases are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This covers:

- the fixed grids in `GRAM Suite 2.0/simulation/GRAM/static_grids/` (`*.jls`);
- named preset grids published as release assets, for example
  `odyssey_p20_frozen_v1-1.0.0.tar.gz`, including the recipe and provenance
  files packed with them;
- named near-surface preset payloads published as release assets, for example
  `mars_global_near_surface_p20_frozen_v1-1.0.0.tar.gz`, including the recipe,
  provenance and support files packed with them.

**Attribution.** Cite the grid by name and version, or by its SHA256 (for
example "Space-FALCON Lab, odyssey_p20_frozen_v1 version 1.0.0"), and credit
NASA's GRAM Suite and the relevant planetary model (for example, Mars-GRAM).

**Notice.** These grids and payloads are output of NASA's Global Reference
Atmospheric Model (GRAM) Suite, run by the Space-FALCON Lab. They contain no
GRAM software or libraries. Apart from the terrain component described below,
they contain no GRAM model input data. NASA has not reviewed or endorsed them.
GRAM Suite itself is distributed by NASA under its own terms (NASA Software
Catalog, MFS-33888-1).

**Terrain component of near-surface payloads.** A near-surface payload stores
Mars-GRAM's surface height and areoid radius at the model's own 0.5-degree MOLA
terrain lattice nodes. At those nodes, the values equal the terrain input that
GRAM distributes. They derive from NASA's Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA)
data. The product needs them so that its near-surface regime matches the native
model exactly. When using them, also credit MOLA: Smith, D. E., et al. (2001),
Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter: Experiment summary after the first year of global
mapping of Mars, J. Geophys. Res., 106(E10), 23689-23722,
doi:10.1029/2000JE001364. No other GRAM input data is included.

**Not covered.** Scenario asset archives, for example
`odyssey_p20_scenario_v1-1.0.0.tar.gz`, redistribute NASA/JPL NAIF SPICE kernels
and Mars gravity coefficients unchanged under their original terms; their
notices are included in the archives. The wrapper code is MIT licensed (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)).
