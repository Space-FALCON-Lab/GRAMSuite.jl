# Terms for generated atmosphere grids

The atmosphere grids generated by the Space-FALCON Lab and distributed in this
repository or its releases are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This covers:

- the fixed grids in `GRAM Suite 2.0/simulation/GRAM/static_grids/` (`*.jls`);
- named preset grids published as release assets, for example
  `odyssey_p20_frozen_v1-1.0.0.tar.gz`, including the recipe and provenance
  files packed with them.

**Attribution.** Cite the grid by name and version, or by its SHA256 (for
example "Space-FALCON Lab, odyssey_p20_frozen_v1 version 1.0.0"), and credit
NASA's GRAM Suite and the relevant planetary model (for example, Mars-GRAM).

**Notice.** These grids are output of NASA's Global Reference Atmospheric Model
(GRAM) Suite, run by the Space-FALCON Lab. They contain no GRAM software,
libraries or model input data. NASA has not reviewed or endorsed them. GRAM
Suite itself is distributed by NASA under its own terms (NASA Software Catalog,
MFS-33888-1).

**Not covered.** Scenario asset archives, for example
`odyssey_p20_scenario_v1-1.0.0.tar.gz`, redistribute NASA/JPL NAIF SPICE kernels
and Mars gravity coefficients unchanged under their original terms; their
notices are included in the archives. The wrapper code is MIT licensed (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)).
